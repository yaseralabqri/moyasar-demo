Cannot fund an AFT

STC Pay and Google Pay do not support Account Funding Transactions, so they are left out of methods on purpose. Card, Apple Pay and Samsung Pay only. The merchant entity also has to be enabled for AFT or the API rejects the payment.

What happens on a 3-D Secure card

Moyasar returns the payment with source.transaction_url and sends the customer to the bank's own challenge page — an SMS code or the bank app. The form does this for you; there is nothing to build. The customer comes back to your callback_url afterwards, which is why the order must be confirmed from the server and never from the URL.
